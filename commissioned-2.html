Energy Grid Analysis for Real Estate Projects

2026 VORM
Data Analysis / Risk Assessment

What happens when we urgently need new homes, but the electricity grid cannot keep up?

The Netherlands faces a growing housing shortage, while electricity grid congestion is becoming another obstacle to residential development. During my work for VORM, I explored how this could affect their housing projects, bringing together scattered information about grid capacity, connection requirements and individual developments.

A questionnaire was developed, covering 49 residential developments and combined the responses with spatial data and technical criteria. This helped to identify approximately 13 projects that required closer investigation, giving VORM a clearer picture of potential risks and where coordination with grid operators was most needed.

What interested me most was the connection between spatial development and energy infrastructure. Grid congestion may seem like a technical issue, but it can directly affect whether new homes can be built. I enjoyed making these complex dependencies visible at the scale of individual projects.
