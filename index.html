BIOGRAPHY

I’m an urban researcher and designer. I study how places work and how people experience them, then turn what I find into visuals, tools and concepts that others can use.

I combine spatial analysis, participatory research and visual design. I also design tools that help people take part in planning. In my master’s thesis, I studied how stakeholders collaborated on rooftop extensions in Rotterdam. I found that bringing people together did not lead to shared commitments, because those with the most influence over implementation could also afford to wait.

I am currently in the process of relocating to London and am actively seeking professional opportunities in the area.
