Ideation for an Immersive Urban Futures Game

2025 CIIIC Innovation Impact Challenge MAD Movement MoMoLab

Developed through a collaboration between MAD Movement and MoMoLab, this project began as part of the 2025 CIIIC Innovation Impact Challenge on urban development. I joined MAD Movement during the initial open call and contributed to the early concept-development phase.

The idea was to create an immersive, collaborative game that would allow policymakers to explore the long-term consequences of urban development decisions. Drawing on future scenarios for the Netherlands towards 2050, we imagined an experience in which participants enter a circular installation of digital screens, encounter policy dilemmas and explore different possible futures together.

My work focused on researching existing immersive experiences, exploring initial concepts and developing spatial sketches of the installation and participant journey. These drawings helped communicate how the experience could unfold, from entering the space to interacting with future scenarios and reflecting on collective decisions.
