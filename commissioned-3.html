Ideation for an Immersive Urban Futures Game

2025 CIIIC Innovation Impact Challenge MAD Movement MoMoLab
Game Design / Concept Design / Visualisation

What if policymakers could step inside the future consequences of their decisions?

This was the starting point for an idea developed by MAD Movement and MoMoLab as part of the 2025 CIIIC Innovation Impact Challenge. The ambition was to create an immersive game in which policymakers could explore different futures for Dutch cities towards 2050, not individually, but together.

I joined MAD Movement during the initial open call and helped explore what such an experience could look and feel like. I researched existing immersive installations, sketched spatial concepts and imagined how participants might move through the experience: entering a circular space surrounded by screens, encountering future scenarios, discussing difficult choices and reflecting on their collective decisions.

What I enjoyed most was translating a fairly abstract idea into something people could actually picture. The sketches weren't finished designs; they were a way to ask questions, test possibilities and give the team something concrete to discuss.

The concept has since developed into Stad op het Spel, an immersive experience by MAD Movement and MoMoLab that is being presented at Dutch Design Week 2026. It's exciting to see how an idea I contributed to during its early development has grown into a public experience.
