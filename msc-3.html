Just Energy Transition — Bridging Communities and Institutions

2025 MSc MADE Energie Lab Zuidoost Stichting EGK

How can an energy transition be just if the people most affected struggle to be heard?

During a five-month Living Lab, we worked with Energie Lab Zuidoost and Stichting EGK Centre to explore why the energy transition in Amsterdam Zuidoost was progressing more slowly than hoped. In a neighbourhood shaped by social inequalities and a complex history of relationships with institutions, we wanted to understand what was actually standing in the way and how we could help it move forward

Through fieldwork and interviews, we explored the relationships between residents, community organisations and institutions. We found that trust was a major challenge, shaped by both historical experiences and present-day institutional structures. We also learned about the importance of local *bridge builders*: people who connect communities with institutions, often without receiving the recognition or support their work deserves.

Co-creation and dialogue

We organised three co-creation sessions with community bridge builders and institutional stakeholders. We created space to discuss challenges around trust, accountability and access to resources, allowing different perspectives to be heard. In one of the sessions we gathered insights for developing a stakeholder network map. In this map we gave every stakeholder a spot, so people can find each other and tried to visualise the relationships between them. We also produced an explanatory video, of which two excerpts are shown here.

The project strengthened my interest in the social side of urban transitions, where relationships, community knowledge and trust are just as important as technical solutions.
