Just Energy Transition — Bridging Communities and Institutions

2025 MSc MADE Energie Lab Zuidoost Stichting EGK

How can an energy transition be just if the people most affected struggle to be heard?

During my MSc MADE, I worked in a team of five with Energie Lab Zuidoost to explore the relationships between residents, community organisations and institutions in Amsterdam Zuidoost. Through fieldwork and interviews, we learned about the importance of local bridge builders: people who connect communities with institutions, often without receiving the recognition or support their work deserves.

Building on these insights, we organised three co-creation sessions with community bridge builders and institutional stakeholders. We created space to discuss challenges around trust, accountability and access to resources, allowing different perspectives to be heard.

Alongside the research and workshops, we developed a stakeholder network map to visualise the relationships between the different actors in Amsterdam Zuidoost. We also produced an explanatory video, of which two excerpts are shown here, and a project website to communicate our process, findings and recommendations. I particularly enjoyed working on these visual elements, finding ways to make complex relationships and research insights more accessible.

The project strengthened my interest in the social side of urban transitions, where relationships, community knowledge and trust are just as important as technical solutions.
