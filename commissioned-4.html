Interactive Explainers for Dutch Metropolitan Innovations

2025
Additional image

Together with the team, we developed a simple, flexible format for interactive explainers that make complex urban challenges easier to understand. Using PowerPoint, the explainers allow presenters to reveal and animate information step by step, while adapting the content to different audiences.

Topics range from urban mobility and heat islands to water management and data governance, providing DMI with a reusable visual storytelling tool for presentations.
